Shardfall: Pip’s Vault

Help Pip rebuild the Vault

A puzzle adventure for iPhone · Kartal Önel

The story

The Vault has shattered and its shards have rained down across the land. Join Pip, the last Shard Keeper, and bring it back piece by piece.

Every piece has its own power

Sand swaps, slime frees locked pieces, crystals fire beams, potions make a Joker and glass marbles smash the piece below. Raise Pip’s Spires floor by floor and watch your gardens come alive by day and night.
