Shardfall: Pip’s Vault

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 16, 2026 · Kartal Önel

Who we are

Shardfall: Pip’s Vault ("the game") is developed and published by Kartal Önel. This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game on iOS or Android. If you have any question, write to onelcan123@gmail.com.

No account, no personal profile

The game has no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not run our own servers that collect information about you.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, gold, diamonds, bricks, boosters, Pip’s Spires, errands and settings) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general location derived from the IP address (country or city level), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. On iOS the game first asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and only non-personalized ads are requested. You can change this choice at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see or store your payment details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase was completed so the item can be delivered, and the game can restore non-consumable purchases on request.

Fonts

The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When fonts are downloaded, Google receives your IP address as part of the normal web request. No cookies are set by this request.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off ad tracking at any time (see above), reset your advertising identifier in your device settings, and delete all game data by uninstalling the game. If you live in the EU, UK, Turkey (KVKK) or another region with data protection laws, you may ask us about any data related to you by writing to onelcan123@gmail.com. We will answer within 30 days.

Security

Because we do not collect personal information on our own servers, there is no account or profile of yours that could leak from us. Third-party services follow their own security practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Last updated: November 16, 2026.
