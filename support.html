Support

Last updated November 14, 2026 · Kartal Önel

Contact us

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? E-mail onelcan123@gmail.com. Please include your device model, your iOS or Android version and the level number if the problem happens in a level. We usually reply within 2 business days.

I bought something but did not receive it

Close the game completely and open it again. Then open the Shop, scroll to the bottom and tap "Restore purchases". If the item is still missing, e-mail us with the date of the purchase and the receipt from Apple or Google.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On iPhone and iPad, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Budget & history.

How do lives work?

You have up to 5 lives. One life is used when you lose a level or leave it after making a move. A new life comes back every 20 minutes. When you have no lives left you can watch a short video for an extra life.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game?

Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the game removes it. Purchases of No Ads and the Premium Bundle can be restored from the Shop after reinstalling.

How do I stop personalized ads?

On iPhone, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking and turn off tracking for Shardfall. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalized. Buying No Ads removes all ads except videos you choose to watch for rewards.
